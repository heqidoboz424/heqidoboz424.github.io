Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026. This policy applies to the personal tool “Aboba” (home page).

Data the app accesses

With the owner’s consent through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner’s YouTube account for two purposes only:

  • Uploading videos and setting their publication time (scope youtube.upload).
  • Reading basic channel statistics and statistics of videos uploaded through the app (scope youtube.readonly).

How data is used and stored

The data is used only to provide these functions. The OAuth token issued by Google is stored locally on the owner’s own computer. It is not sent to any third party, not sold, not shared, and not used for advertising, analytics or any other purpose.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation the app can no longer access the account.
