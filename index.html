Aboba

It is a personal tool used by its owner to schedule and upload videos to their own YouTube channel and to view that channel’s basic statistics (subscribers, views, number of videos).

What the app does

  • Uploads videos to the owner’s YouTube channel and schedules their publication, using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads the channel’s public statistics and the statistics of videos uploaded through the app.

Google user data

The app requests two YouTube permissions: uploading videos and read-only access to channel data. Details on how this data is used and stored are in the Privacy Policy.
